Picso Technical & Licensing FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Picso.

Is Picso a standalone PHP script or a WordPress theme + plugin?

Picso is a dedicated WordPress theme plus the Picso Core plugin—giving you the best of both worlds: effortless WordPress hosting, free Elementor visual editing (170+ custom widgets), and an enterprise-grade marketplace engine (8 asset formats, cryptographic PDF licensing, Stripe Connect payouts, and S3/R2 storage).

Do I need Elementor Pro to customize Picso pages?

No. Picso's theme builder works with the free Elementor plugin and includes 170+ widgets and 110 ready-made designs. Even without Elementor installed, Picso runs cleanly on its built-in high-performance templates.

How are licenses issued and verified for downloaded stock assets?

Every asset download automatically issues a unique license ID bound to the buyer's project name and generates a numbered PDF certificate. Anyone can verify a license on the public verification page—and it remains valid even if the asset is later removed.
